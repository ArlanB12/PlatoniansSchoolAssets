Accountability Tiers

Automatic restrictions applied based on how overdue a borrower's item is.

Tier 0
Clean — No overdue items. Full borrowing privileges.
Tier 1
Warning (1–2 days overdue) — Login allowed with a warning banner. Can still borrow.
Tier 2
Suspended (3–6 days overdue) — Login allowed but borrow requests are blocked.
Tier 3
Restricted (7–13 days overdue) — Login is blocked. Must return item and contact Administrator.
Tier 4
Escalated (14+ days overdue) — Account locked. Escalation alert sent to all Administrators.

Administrators can override a user's tier restriction from the Users page.
